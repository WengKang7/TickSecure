# Ticket collection artwork — version 1

Created with the built-in image-generation tool for TickSecure's website collection. These original local PNG assets do not change NFT metadata, minting, wallets or blockchain ownership.

## Saved assets

- `cosmic-encore-v1.png` — chrome astronaut, colourful headphones and a cosmic festival stage.
- `tropical-frequency-v1.png` — tropical garden, golden disco ball, flowers and an open-air stage.
- `electric-horizons-v1.png` — chrome speakers and headphones, flowing sound waves and a floating concert platform.

`assets/js/collectible-art.js` assigns one of these images using the ticket ID. The assignment stays consistent on refresh and follows the same ticket after transfer. All event, category, seat, ticket-status and mint-status labels come from the buyer's existing records. Imagery may repeat; the ticket details remain distinct.

## Final prompt set

Shared brief: Create an original, premium NFT ticket artwork for a music festival collection. Portrait 4:5, full bleed. No text, typography, logos, QR codes, frames, device or mockup. Sophisticated collectible digital illustration mixing clean sculptural 3D forms and graphic editorial shapes. Crisp shapes, high detail, vibrant attractive colour and polished gallery quality. Main subject in the upper two thirds. Bottom quarter transitions into dark coloured scenery for the website's ticket information overlay.

1. **Cosmic Encore:** A joyful futuristic chrome astronaut with oversized orange-and-pink headphones floating above a stylized tiny concert stage, surrounded by glossy cobalt, lilac and hot-pink orbital ribbons and radiant yellow stars. Rich electric blue, coral orange, fuchsia and pale lavender; deep cobalt and violet lower scenery.
2. **Tropical Frequency:** A surreal tropical music garden: luminous lime and emerald palm leaves, coral-pink sculptural flowers, a glossy golden disco ball above an intimate geometric festival stage, rounded purple mountains and a turquoise river curving towards the viewer. Rich turquoise, emerald, tangerine, coral pink and butter yellow; deep teal and green lower scenery.
3. **Electric Horizons:** A surreal monumental chrome audio speaker and sculptural headphones on a floating concert platform, liquid orange-and-pink sound waves, ultramarine stairways, small reflective silver spheres and a blazing yellow sun. Playful architecture and rhythmic geometric shapes. Rich tangerine, ruby pink, bright ultramarine, silver and golden yellow; deep burgundy and purple lower scenery.

No existing project artwork was overwritten. The original generated files also remain in the image tool's output directory.
